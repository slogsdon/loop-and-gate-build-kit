# Business Context Brief — <project / feature name>

> Fill this in **before** brainstorming or planning. It's the context every
> downstream gate inherits, so it's the highest-leverage thing you'll write on a
> project. Treat it as a form you fill, not an essay you write — a blank you can't
> fill is a signal, not a formatting problem.
>
> This feeds **Gate 0** (should this exist?) and **Gate 1** (who + what's a win?).
> Everything the agent builds downstream is built against what's here. Rush it and
> the pipeline confidently builds the wrong thing, fast.
